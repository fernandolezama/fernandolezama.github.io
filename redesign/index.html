    About & contact

    Fernando Lezama

    Researcher in computational intelligence and energy systems, based in Porto, Portugal.

    I am a researcher at GECAD and LASI, School of Engineering (ISEP), Polytechnic of Porto. My work focuses on computational intelligence and optimization for energy communities, local electricity markets and distributed energy resources.

    Through FAIR-CI, my individual research project supported by FCT’s CEECIND, 7th Edition, I study fairness-aware computational intelligence for optimizing local energy systems.

    I also work with Fabio Castro and Leticia Gomes in ITES-Opt, our Interdisciplinary Team on Energy Systems Optimization.
